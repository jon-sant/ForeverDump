Warcraft Forever Collection Giveaway
To celebrate the announcement of World of Warcraft: Forever, we're giving away one key of Warcraft Forever Collection, which includes access to the Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom Campaign.


Warcraft Forever Collection



Giveaway Information
  • This giveaway is open to everyone. No purchase necessary. Void where prohibited.
  • To enter the giveaway, please reply to this post with your favorite feature from World of Warcraft: Forever.
  • One entry is granted for replying to this post with the requested information. One entry per person.
  • The giveaway will be closed on September 20 at 3:00 PM PDT and the winner will be chosen randomly within one business day.
  • The winner will be contacted by PM on this website.
  • The winner has 72 hours to respond before another winner will be selected to replace them.
  • World of Warcraft: Forever officially launches on November 4, 2026.
  • Thanks to Blizzard for the giveaway opportunity! #WoW_Partner
