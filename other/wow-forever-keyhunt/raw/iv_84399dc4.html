WoW Forever Beta Keys Are Now a McDonald’s Reward in Australia

by Tharid

McDonald’s Australia, in collaboration with Blizzard Entertainment, has unveiled a creative way to get into the WoW Forever Beta: Redeem 1,000 MyMacca’s Rewards points in the official MyMacca’s app, and you shall be rewarded with a Beta key.

How to Get A WoW Forever Beta Key Through MyMacca’s

Here’s how to get a WoW Forever Beta key through McDonald’s Australia’s MyMacca’s app:

  • Open the Australian MyMacca’s app and select Rewards & Deals -> Partner Rewards
  • Redeem 1,000 points, copy the code, and activate it through your Battle.net account

No WoW Subscription Required – but Keys Are Limited!

Also, keep the following detals in mind when thinking about snagging a WoW Forever Beta key this way:

  • A Battle.net account is required, but no active WoW subscription or game time is needed!
  • As McDonald’s states, keys are “available while stocks last“, limited to one key per MyMacca’s account and WoW account.
  • Claim and activate the code by October 20, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. AEDT. The beta ends October 22, and character progress will not carry over to launch. These restrictions also appear in McDonald’s official FAQ.
